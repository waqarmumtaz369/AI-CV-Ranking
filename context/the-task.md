**Revolutionizing Recruitment at Tkxel: A Generative AI-Powered CV Parsing and Ranking System**
 
Tkxel is set to streamline its talent acquisition process with the development of an innovative CV parsing and ranking system powered by Generative AI. This intelligent platform will automate and enhance the initial stages of recruitment by providing a comprehensive and efficient analysis of candidate profiles.  
 
At its core, the system will leverage advanced Natural Language Processing (NLP) to intelligently parse and extract crucial information from resumes.  
 
A key innovation of this project is its ability to cross-reference and validate the information presented in a CV against the candidate's LinkedIn profile. This verification process will help ensure the authenticity of the candidate's listed experiences and qualifications. 
 
Furthermore, the system will accurately calculate the total years of experience by intelligently summing up the durations of all listed work experiences, providing recruiters with a quick and reliable overview of a candidate's career timeline.  
 
A central feature of the platform is its sophisticated skill-matching capability. Given a job description, the system will generate a detailed matching matrix, highlighting the alignment between the required skills and the candidate's qualifications as mentioned in their CV. This matrix will offer a granular view of how well a candidate's skill set meets the specific needs of a role.  
 
Ultimately, the project aims to rank candidates based on the number of matched skills, allowing recruiters to prioritize the most suitable applicants efficiently. By automating these critical yet time-consuming tasks, Tkxel's Generative AI-powered CV parsing and ranking system will empower the HR team to focus on engaging with top-tier talent, thereby accelerating the hiring process and improving the overall quality of hires. This AI-driven approach will not only enhance efficiency but also promote a more data-driven and objective evaluation of candidates.
