AI Engineer

Requirements

Experience: 2–3 years in AI, machine learning, or a related field OR a strong academic background with notable AI/ML projects
Strong understanding of algorithms, data structures, and statistical concepts
Proficiency in Python (or similar programming languages)
Familiarity with machine learning frameworks such as TensorFlow, PyTorch, or Scikit-learn (preferred)
Strong analytical thinking, problem-solving skills, and curiosity for new challenges
Ability to work independently, learn quickly, and adapt to evolving technologies
Genuine passion for AI and its transformative potential for businesses. 

Bonus Skills (Preferred, Not Required)

Experience with Natural Language Processing, Computer Vision, or Reinforcement Learning
Knowledge of cloud services (AWS, GCP, or Azure) and ML deployment pipelines
Familiarity with MLOps practices and tools.
